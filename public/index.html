How The WasteWise Lifecycle Works

Every collection is tracked through an auditable, five-stage database workflow ensuring zero illegal dumping and maximum resource recovery.

1
1. Requested

Citizen schedules pickup with category, quantity, and address.

2
2. Assigned

Admin assigns an active collector and nearest authorized facility.

3
3. Collected

Collector weighs waste on-site and records digital collection log.

4
4. Delivered

Collector drops waste at recycling center intake bay with delivery notes.

5
5. Completed

Facility verifies received weight, rejects contaminants, and records recycling.

Source Segregation

Accepted Waste Streams

Separate your recyclables at source to maximize processing efficiency.

Organic Waste

Kitchen scraps, fruit/vegetable peels, leftover food, horticultural green waste, fallen leaves. Processed into organic compost.

Plastic Recyclables

Clean PET beverage bottles, HDPE milk jugs, hard plastic containers, packaging wrap. Flaked and recycled into synthetic yarn.

Paper & Cardboard

Corrugated shipping cartons, newspapers, old books, clean paper sheets. Pulped and remanufactured into recycled craft paper.

Glass Containers

Clear and colored bottles, jars, glass food containers. Crushed into cullet and melted to produce fresh glass packaging.

Metal & Scrap

Aluminum beverage cans, tin food cans, iron utensils, copper wiring scrap. Melted with 95% energy savings over virgin ore.

Electronic Waste

Old smartphones, obsolete laptops, chargers, cables, circuit boards. Processed in certified facilities for safe heavy metal recovery.
