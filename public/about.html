About WasteWise Portal

Web Technology IA3 Project — Smart Municipal Waste Management & Circular Economy

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Rapid urbanization and growing consumer waste generate immense municipal strain. Traditional waste management often suffers from poor segregation at source, unverified contractor collections, rampant illegal open-air dumping, and lack of transparency between citizens and recycling processors.

WasteWise bridges this gap by providing an end-to-end digital lifecycle connecting Citizens, municipal Collectors, authorized Recycling Centers, and municipal Administrators. Every pickup is authenticated, tracked, weighed, and audited.

2. The 5-Stage Closed-Loop Lifecycle

Requested Citizen creates pickup request with category, estimated weight, and preferred slot.
Assigned Admin assigns an active on-duty collector and appropriate licensed recycling facility.
Collected Collector arrives on-site, weighs materials on digital scales, and logs actual collected weight.
Delivered Collector drops off waste at the designated facility intake bay and records drop-off notes.
Completed Facility inspects intake, separates rejects, logs net recycled weight, and records processing outcome.
