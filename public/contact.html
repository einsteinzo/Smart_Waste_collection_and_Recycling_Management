Frequently Asked Questions

Simply register as a citizen or log in, then click "Request Pickup" on your dashboard. Choose your waste category (Plastic, E-Waste, Paper, Organic, etc.), enter an estimated quantity, select your preferred date/time slot, and confirm. An administrator will assign a collector to your neighborhood.

Yes! You can cancel any request as long as its status is Requested or Assigned (before physical collection takes place). Once the collector has picked up and weighed the materials on-site, the request cannot be cancelled.

Citizens enter an estimated quantity when booking. When the collector arrives, they use a certified portable digital scale to record the actual collected weight. Upon delivery, the recycling facility weighs and screens the materials, recording the net recycled weight and any contaminated rejects.

For security and regulatory compliance, collectors and recycling facilities are vetted and onboarded directly by municipal Administrators. Public registration is reserved for citizens.
