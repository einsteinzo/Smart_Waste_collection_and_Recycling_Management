Source Segregation & Recycling Guide

Proper source segregation enables 90%+ material recovery and prevents landfill accumulation.

Green Bin: Wet / Organic
Compostable Biomass
What Goes In:
  • Fruit and vegetable peels & cuttings
  • Cooked and uncooked leftover food
  • Coffee grounds and tea bags
  • Garden leaves, grass cuttings, twigs
  • Egg shells and nutshells
DO NOT INCLUDE: Plastic wrap, milk pouches, diapers, treated wood.
Blue Bin: Dry Recyclables
Plastics & Polymers
What Goes In:
  • PET beverage and water bottles
  • HDPE detergent and shampoo jugs
  • Clean rinsed milk sachets and pouches
  • Rigid plastic food containers
  • Bubble wrap and clean LDPE bags
PREPARATION: Rinse out milk or food residue and allow to dry before binning.
Yellow Bin: Paper & Board
Clean Cellulosic Fiber
What Goes In:
  • Corrugated cardboard shipping cartons
  • Newspapers, magazines, and periodicals
  • Office print paper and notebook sheets
  • Cereal and food paper boxes (dry)
DO NOT INCLUDE: Greasy pizza boxes, used paper napkins, waxed coffee cups.
Grey Bin: Glass Containers
Bottles & Jars
What Goes In:
  • Clear, brown, and green beverage bottles
  • Glass food and condiment jars
  • Cosmetic and perfume bottles
DO NOT INCLUDE: Mirrors, window panes, incandescent bulbs, Pyrex bakeware.
Dark Bin: Metal & Scrap
Ferrous & Non-Ferrous
What Goes In:
  • Aluminum soda & beverage cans
  • Tin and steel canned food containers
  • Metal jar lids and bottle crowns
  • Clean aluminum kitchen foil
  • Old steel/iron utensils and wire
PREPARATION: Flatten cans to save collection space in your home bin.
Red Bin: Domestic E-Waste
Special Certified Processing
What Goes In:
  • Defunct mobile phones and tablets
  • Broken laptop adapters, chargers, cables
  • Household batteries and dry cells
  • Computer peripherals (mouse, keyboards)
WARNING: Never throw batteries in regular municipal bins due to fire risks.
